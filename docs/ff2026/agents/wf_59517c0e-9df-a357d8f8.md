# 0. Read this first — what the corpus actually is

I read all 5,610 lines. The single most important finding about the data itself:

**There is no complete ADP table anywhere in the corpus.** All 217 search blocks are *search-result summaries*, and the overwhelming majority end with some version of "the full list isn't displayed in these results — visit FantasyPros/ESPN/FFC directly." What exists instead is roughly **95 scattered player-ADP data points** harvested from a dozen *different* sources on at least **four incompatible numbering scales**. I have assembled those into a board. I have not invented anything to fill gaps.

Four scales are provably in play, and mixing them is the #1 way to misprice a player today:

| Scale | Example | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Decimal overall pick** (most common) | Gibbs 1.0, Barkley 14.3, Bucky Irving 54.8 | F1:41, F1:47, F2:1229 |
| **B. `round.pick` notation** | Chase "1.04" = 4th overall; Hampton "2.08" = 19th overall; Hall "3.08" = 32nd overall | F1:2342, F1:2323, F1:2767 |
| **C. Positional rank only** | "Skattebo RB18", "Diggs WR49", "Ferguson TE12" | F1:321, F1:1863, F2:58 |
| **D. Site-specific overall rank** (Field Yates / Mike Clay ESPN) | "#25 (WR9) Nabers", "#33 (WR13) Egbuka" | F1:2420, F1:2421 |

Scale B looks like scale A but is off by up to 9 picks (McCaffrey "2.03" vs "5.8"). I flag every instance below.

**Also critical: the corpus contains ZERO kicker data and exactly ONE defense.** More in §6.

# 2. The 14-team, 13-round conversion

### The arithmetic (mine, UNVERIFIED as to corpus, but it is just division)

The corpus ADP is overwhelmingly **12-team** — Fantasy Football Calculator is explicitly labeled "12 Teams All Players" (F1:1606) and generated from "2,828 mock drafts between August 17, 2026 and August 22, 2026" (F1:2123); FantasyPros mocks used "six bench slots" (F1:2127). **Every round label in every article you will read today is wrong for your league.**

An overall ADP of *N* maps to:
- 12-team round = ⌈N/12⌉
- **14-team round = ⌈N/14⌉**

| Your round | Picks | Players with ADP ≤ this pick |
|---|---|---|
| **R1** | 1–14 | Gibbs, Bijan, Chase, Nacua, JSN, McCaffrey, J.Taylor, Achane, Cook, Jeanty, Hampton, Kyren, Barkley, Lamb |
| **R2** | 15–28 | Jefferson, Henry, ARSB, Chase Brown, London, Love, Josh Allen, **Bowers, McBride**, Rice, Pickens, A.J. Brown, K.Walker, Nico Collins |
| **R3** | 29–42 | Breece Hall, Nabers, Javonte Williams, G.Wilson, Jacobs, Egbuka, Loveland, Lamar Jackson, McConkey, McMillan, Skattebo(RB18) |
| **R4** | 43–56 | Drake Maye, McLaurin, Waddle, Bucky Irving, TreVeyon Henderson, Tyler Warren, Burden, Tee Higgins, Montgomery(RB24), Etienne(RB25) |
| **R5** | 57–70 | Jayden Daniels, D.J. Moore, Jalen Hurts, LaPorta |
| **R6** | 71–84 | Kyle Pitts, Tucker Kraft, Jameson Williams(WR24), Brian Thomas Jr.(WR31) |

| **R9–R13** | 113–182 | **Corpus coverage collapses here.** Only named with ranks: Woody Marks RB43, Tyrone Tracy RB44, Chris Rodriguez RB45, Tank Bigsby RB47/ADP 172, Stefon Diggs WR49, Chig Okonkwo TE21-24, Gunnar Helm TE30, Justin Herbert QB15 |

### The three round-label traps this creates

1. **"Round 2 TE" is a Round 2 TE here too — but the window is 4 picks narrower.** Bowers/McBride at ADP 20–28 sit at the *very back* of your R2 (picks 15–28). In a 12-team league they'd straddle the 2/3 turn. **CONFIRMED-adjacent:** "You'll see Fantasy managers willing to take the chance on McBride and Bowers by the back end of Round 2" F1:1808.
2. **"Round 3 pick" advice is now Round 2–3.** Love is called "an aggressive third-round selection" (F1:163) at ADP 25 — in your league ADP 25 is **Round 2, pick 11**. Breece Hall's "3.08" (F1:2767) is 32nd overall = your **Round 3, pick 4**.
3. **Everything from ~pick 56 down moves up a full round.** Tyler Warren at 55.9 is "end of Round 5" in 12-team advice (F1:1810) but is your **Round 4**. Kraft at 76.3 is 12-team R7, your **R6**.

### The 14-team compression that actually matters

**CONFIRMED, and it is the sharpest single line in the corpus:** *"The top 12 running backs are expected to be gone by the end of Round 2 in most drafts this year. The top 10-11 RBs should all be off the board by picks 15 to 20."* (F1:1734)

That sentence was written for 12 teams. Picks 15–20 is your **Round 2, picks 1–6**. So in a 14-team room, RB1–RB11 are gone before your R2 is half over, and RB12 is gone by pick ~24. **UNVERIFIED inference:** with 14 drafters instead of 12 chasing the same 12 backs, expect that to happen 1–3 picks *earlier*, not later.

Corroborating: *"the RB24 is going on average at pick 55 in PPR formats, which would be the highest ADP for RB24 since 2020"* (F2:1769). Pick 55 = your **Round 4, pick 13**. So RB1–RB24 are all gone by the end of your Round 4.
