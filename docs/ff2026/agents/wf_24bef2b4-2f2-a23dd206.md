## 4. HALF-PPR + 6-PT RUSH TD: WHO GAINS, WHO LOSES — QUANTIFIED

### The exchange rates in YOUR league

| Unit | Points | Equivalent to |
|---|---|---|
| 1 reception | **0.5** | 5 rushing yards |
| 1 rushing TD | **6.0** | **12 receptions** or 60 rushing yards |
| 1 receiving TD | 6.0 | 12 receptions |
| 1 fumble lost | **−2.0** | 4 receptions or 20 rushing yards |
| **A 70-catch season** | **35 pts** | **≈ 5.8 rushing TDs** *(in full PPR it would be 11.7)* |
| 10 carries for 45 yds | 4.5 | 9 receptions |

**The headline number: half-PPR cuts the reception premium exactly in half, which means a 70-reception season is worth ~5.8 rushing TDs instead of ~11.7.** That is the entire strategic delta.

### Format retention: same player, three scoring systems (2025 actuals)

| Player (2025 real line) | Standard | **Half-PPR** | Full PPR | Half as % of Full |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **CMC** (311/1,202/10 + 102/924/7) | 314.6 | **365.6** | 416.6 | **87.8%** |
| **Chase Brown** (1,019/6 + 69/437/5) | 211.6 | **246.1** | 280.6 | **87.7%** |
| **Derrick Henry** (1,595/16 rushing only) | 255.5 | **255.5** | 255.5 | **100%** |

**Derrick Henry generated 255.5 points from rushing alone — more than Chase Brown's entire half-PPR season (246.1).** That's the format in one comparison. A 100-catch back retains 88% of his full-PPR value; a rushing-only back retains 100%.

### Archetype models (per-game, this league's exact scoring)

| Archetype | Line | Standard | **Half-PPR** | Full PPR |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Bell-cow dual threat** (Gibbs, CMC, Achane, Bijan) | 17 att/78 yds/0.55 TD, 4.0 rec/32 yds | 14.3 | **16.3** | 18.3 |
| **B. Volume/TD grinder** (Henry, Montgomery, Judkins, JCM, Taylor) | 20 att/88 yds/0.75 TD, 1.2 rec/8 yds | 14.1 | **14.7** | 15.3 |
| **C. Committee early-down** (Dobbins, Hubbard, Stevenson, Allgeier) | 12 att/50 yds/0.35 TD, 1.0 rec/7 yds | 7.8 | **8.3** | 8.8 |
| **D. Passing-down specialist** (Sampson, Gainwell, R. White, Warren, K. Mitchell) | 5 att/21 yds/0.1 TD, 4.5 rec/38 yds | 6.5 | **8.75** | 11.0 |

**Read the deltas:**
- **A over B:** +3.0 PPG in full PPR (+19.6%) → **+1.6 PPG in half-PPR (+10.9%)** → +0.2 PPG in standard. **Half-PPR erases 47% of the elite receiver's edge over the grinder.**
- **B over D:** +4.3 PPG in full PPR → **+5.95 PPG in half-PPR** → +7.6 in standard. **The volume grinder's edge over the passing-down specialist is 38% wider in half-PPR than in full PPR.**

### 📈 GAIN VALUE vs. FULL PPR (draft these UP relative to the ADP board)

1. **Derrick Henry (BAL)** — biggest single beneficiary in football. 16 rushing TDs = 96 points that are format-agnostic. His ADP of 15.7 is a *full-PPR* price.
2. **David Montgomery (HOU)** — RB24 ADP for a clear lead back whose value is carries + goal line. Textbook Archetype B.
3. **Jonathan Taylor (IND)** — 13 projected rushing TDs = 78 points untouched by scoring format. The 45 projected receptions cost him only 22.5 pts vs full PPR.
4. **Quinshon Judkins (CLE)** — losing passing downs to Sampson costs him **half** of what it would in full PPR while he keeps 100% of the goal line.
5. **Jacory Croskey-Merritt (WAS)** — 8 rushing TDs as a rookie 7th-rounder = 48 points, format-neutral. His near-zero receiving role barely dings him here.
6. **Ashton Jeanty (LV)**, **James Cook (BUF)**, **Saquon Barkley (PHI)**, **Rhamondre Stevenson (NE)**, **Tyler Allgeier (ARI)**, **Chuba Hubbard (CAR)**.

### 📉 LOSE VALUE vs. FULL PPR (fade relative to the ADP board)

1. **Pure receiving specialists** — **Dylan Sampson (CLE)**, **Kenneth Gainwell (TB)**, **Rachaad White (WAS)**, **Jaylen Warren (PIT)**, **Keaton Mitchell (LAC)**, **Kendre Miller (NO)**. A 60-catch/40-carry back loses 30 points (~1.8 PPG) vs full PPR and gains nothing back. In a 2RB+FLEX league these are **not startable**.
2. **RJ Harvey (DEN)** — his differentiator over Dobbins is 356 receiving yards and pass-catching; half-PPR taxes the reception half of that.
3. **TreVeyon Henderson (NE)** — assigned the 2nd/3rd-and-long passing role while Stevenson gets short yardage. He's paying the reception tax *and* forfeiting the 6-pt TDs.
4. **Woody Marks (HOU)**, **Tyrone Tracy (NYG)** if used as the passing-down back, **Aaron Jones (MIN)** in his stated passing-situations role.
5. **CMC / Gibbs / Achane** lose the most raw points (CMC gives up 51 points vs full PPR) — but they're so far ahead in total volume that their *rank* barely moves. Don't over-fade them; just don't pay a full-PPR premium for the receptions specifically.

### 📈 GAIN VALUE vs. STANDARD
Everything in Archetype A and D. Gibbs, CMC, Achane, Chase Brown, and Sampson/Gainwell types are all materially better than in standard. **Half-PPR is the midpoint — you get 50% of the receiving-back bonus and 100% of the TD-grinder bonus. That is why the RIGHT read for this league is "TD-and-volume first, receptions as a tiebreaker."**

## 6. THE 4-BENCH-SPOT PROBLEM: WHO'S TOO RISKY, WHO'S SAFEST

### The math of your roster

13 spots. QB + TE + D/ST + K = **4 spots gone**. That leaves **9 for RB + WR**, against 5 starting RB/WR slots (RB, RB, WR, WR, FLEX). Realistic build: **5 RB / 4 WR** or **4 RB / 5 WR**. You will draft 13 rounds, and rounds 12–13 are K and D/ST — so you get **11 skill players, total.**

**You can carry at most 2–3 RBs beyond your weekly starters. Handcuffing is off the table.**

### Three league rules that compound this — and they all point the same direction

1. **Waiver order RESETS WEEKLY BY INVERSE STANDINGS.** This is the most important non-scoring rule in your league. Every Tuesday, the *worst* teams pick first. If you are good, **you will essentially never win a contested waiver claim on a breakout RB.** This destroys the premise of Zero-RB and of "I'll grab the handcuff when the starter goes down." Everything about this league says: **pay for volume in the draft, because you cannot buy it later.**
2. **Only 4 of 10–12 teams make the playoffs**, seeded by total points as tiebreaker. You need a high weekly ceiling *and* a floor, from Week 1. There is no "limp in at .500" path.
3. **Two-week playoff matchups.** With 4 teams and 2-week rounds, your championship almost certainly spans **Weeks 17–18**. **Week 18 is when contenders rest starters.** RBs on teams likely to lock a seed early carry hidden championship-week risk; RBs on middling/bad teams that play everyone in Week 18 carry hidden championship-week *value*. (Confirm your exact playoff weeks in the league settings before you draft.)

### Recommended draft shape

**12-TEAM (picks through ~#132; RB40 becomes a bye-week starter; waivers are barren):**
- **Rounds 1–2: two of {Bijan, Gibbs, CMC, Taylor, Jeanty, Achane, Cook, Chase Brown, Henry, Saquon}.** Anchor-RB is correct here, and the weekly inverse-standings waiver reset is the reason why. **Do not run Zero-RB in this league** — you have neither the bench spots to stash six lottery tickets nor the waiver priority to convert them.
- Rounds 3–5: WR, WR, and your TE/QB if value falls.
- Rounds 6–8: **RB3 with a standalone role** — Montgomery, Etienne, Croskey-Merritt, Pollard, Walker III if he slides.
- Rounds 9–11: WR depth + one contingent RB with flex value (Corum, Tracy, Bigsby).
- Rounds 12–13: D/ST, K.

**10-TEAM (only ~25–30 RB starting slots league-wide; RB30 is genuinely on waivers):**
- Replacement-level RB is much cheaper, so **Hero-RB is viable**: one elite anchor in Round 1, then hammer WR through Rounds 2–4, and take your RB2/RB3 from the Montgomery / Etienne / Pollard / Croskey-Merritt / Judkins band in Rounds 5–8.
- **Still avoid every declared committee.** With only 4 bench spots, the cost of a wrong guess is the same in a 10-teamer; you just have a slightly better shot at fixing it — and even then only if you're near the bottom of the standings, which is where the weekly waiver reset puts the good picks.

**One roster-construction crossover to be aware of:** your D/ST scoring also grades **yards allowed** (−7 for 550+, +5 for under 100), which normally argues for streaming two defenses. **With four bench spots, don't.** Draft one D/ST in the last two rounds and use every bench slot on RB/WR — the RB roster crunch is the binding constraint on this team.
